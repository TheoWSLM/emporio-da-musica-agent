## Findings and treatment

### 1. Uneven catalog coverage
- **Finding:** Violões: 35, Ukuleles: 15, Guitarras: 5, Baixos: 4, Baterias e Percussão: 3, Teclados e Pianos: 3. Categories without products: Instrumentos de Sopro (Madeiras), Instrumentos de Sopro (Metais), Cordas Orquestrais.
- **Treatment:** Searches in empty categories answer honestly that there is nothing in the catalog.

### 2. Non-active products
- **Finding:** Status distribution: {'active': 62, 'discontinued': 2, 'coming_soon': 1}. Non-active: 113 (discontinued), 130 (coming_soon), 136 (discontinued).
- **Treatment:** Discontinued and coming-soon products are never offered for sale; the agent suggests similar in-stock items.

### 3. Active products without stock
- **Finding:** Products 96 are 'active' with stock_quantity = 0.
- **Treatment:** Availability is derived from stock, not from status: shown as temporarily out of stock.

### 4. Several promotions for the same product
- **Finding:** product 93: 2 promotions; product 96: 2 promotions; product 99: 2 promotions
- **Treatment:** Only active promotions count; if several were active, the largest discount would win.

### 5. Active promotions
- **Finding:** 4 active promotions on products 90, 94, 121, 127; at most one active per product.
- **Treatment:** Promotions have no validity dates: `is_active` is taken as the source of truth.

### 6. Order totals differ from current prices
- **Finding:** order 3: total 3450 vs items x current price 3498; order 20: total 1400 vs items x current price 1488
- **Treatment:** The order total is historical (prices change): it is shown as stored and never recalculated.

### 7. Orders containing products that are not for sale today
- **Finding:** order 3 (delivered) has product 130 (coming_soon); order 14 (pending) has product 130 (coming_soon)
- **Treatment:** Kept as historical facts; documented as a data inconsistency.

### 8. No receipt date on orders
- **Finding:** Orders only have `estimated_delivery`; return windows (§4.1, §4.3) count from the receipt date.
- **Treatment:** For delivered orders the estimated delivery date is assumed to be the receipt date.

### 9. Historical data
- **Finding:** The most recent order is from 2026-03-22; with today's date every return window is closed.
- **Treatment:** The business clock accepts APP_REFERENCE_DATE so that return scenarios can be demonstrated.

### 10. Customers with near-duplicate names
- **Finding:** Bruno Carvalho Martins / Bruno Martins; Diego Fernandes Castro / Diego Castro; Bruno Carvalho Martins / Bruno Carvalho
- **Treatment:** Identity is verified by e-mail or phone, never by name.
